# Cold Read: creative brief for the team

Approved game plan (owner approved 2026-10-04): `docs/Cold-Read-Game-Plan.pdf`. Key points:

- **Game:** Cold Read. A free, dark, serious detective game in the browser (phone and computer),
  English. Each level is one murder case. It trains real skills: noticing details, connecting
  clues, reading human behaviour (lies, body language, motives). Inspired by the *style* of
  The Mentalist, but fully original: never use the show's characters, names, symbols or plots.
- **Player:** Julian Marsh, a former TV psychic whose act was a trick (cold reading plus paid
  researchers digging up private details). He quit after his final show, where he "revealed" a
  woman's secret; weeks later she was found dead, ruled suicide. He now consults for the
  Port Halden Police Department, Major Crimes Unit.
- **Partner:** Detective Sergeant Lena Cruz. By the book, sharp, sceptical of Julian. Handles
  procedure, gives the hints. Dry friction with Julian.
- **Tech expert:** Theo Park, reached by phone/message. Phone records, camera footage, bank
  activity, background checks.
- **City:** Port Halden, invented grey harbour city. Districts: Old Town, The Docks, Hillcrest,
  Eastgate, Financial Quarter.
- **Tone:** dark and serious. Crime scenes realistic but restrained: the victim is visible,
  no close-up gore. Rated 16+.
- **Look:** realistic documents and evidence; people and places illustrated in one consistent
  dark graphic-novel style.
- **Play:** opening sequence → case file → free investigation (scene, people, places, timeline,
  evidence board, notebook, requests to Theo) on an in-game clock → a twist about halfway →
  the player builds the case: killer + motive + method + key evidence. All must hold up.
- **Progress signals** only for real progress ("Lead confirmed", new places unlock, case meter).
- **Hints** are earned (key deductions, side puzzles) and point where to look, never the answer.
- **Wrong case:** the wrong person is charged, with a story consequence; the case can be
  reopened with the same killer but a changed trail.
- **Debrief** after a solved case: what was found and missed, the real technique behind each
  key clue, and a real-life tip.
- **Difficulty:** hard but always fair. Every answer must be reachable from evidence in the
  case; nothing depends on luck.
- **Chapter 1, Case 1** (the pilot) is a standalone case (not part of the hidden-villain
  thread), and should teach the tools while still being genuinely challenging. Target play
  time 25–40 minutes.
