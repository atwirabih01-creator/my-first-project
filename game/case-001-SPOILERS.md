## The true story

Iris Kellan was a claims investigator at Halden Mutual Marine. While working on Petra Lund's claim for her sunken trawler *Grey Petrel*, she noticed the survey report described the wrong engine (a "Volda 6", when the boat had a Brandt 8). Petra then told her no surveyor had ever come aboard, and Iris's suspicion moved from Petra to the survey firm, **Northline Survey Services**. Northline had billed fourteen claims at about fifteen times the market rate, €612,400 in all. Every one was approved and marked "expedite" by her manager, **Graham Whitlock**, Head of Claims. Northline is a mailbox run by Whitlock's brother-in-law Rolf Dahl, and it passes about 70% of every fee to Whitlock's wife Margit.

Iris emailed Compliance (Ruth Achebe) on Tuesday night and booked a confidential meeting for Thursday 09:00, marked "private" in her work calendar. Whitlock can see his team's calendars. On Wednesday afternoon he read the entry and asked Nico about it. He badged out of the lobby at 18:10 so the record would show him leaving. At 18:52 he rang Iris and asked her to come back at eight so he could "explain". He waited until Nico's bike had gone, then came back in through the building's car park door at 19:55; that door is on a separate system from Halden Mutual's lobby. Iris badged in at 19:58. She unlocked her laptop at 20:24 to print the Northline list in the archive. In the archive she refused to back down, and as she turned to leave he struck her once on the back of the head with his Helm Award, a brass ship's wheel 18 cm across on a granite base.

He then set up a false time of death. At 20:51 he used her still-unlocked laptop to schedule a message from her account to her sister for 23:12 ("walking home the long way…"), a time when he would be in plain view at the Harbour Club quiz. He cleaned the carpet with the archive spill kit, wrapped her in a dust sheet and took her down in the car park lift. His car left at 22:04. At the dead-end foot of the Saltmarket Stairs he dragged her up three steps to the unlit lower landing, laid her face down, and took her handbag (with her notebook) to suggest a robbery. Then he drove to the club. The award was found later in his garage at home.

The scene estimate (22:00–01:00) assumed she had lain outside all night, and that error is exactly what the staging needed.

### Opening case file (granted at the start)
`caseFile`: first officer's report, scene log and property inventory, the doctor's preliminary notes, the next-of-kin notification, and the initial contacts list. Together with the longer briefing and victim profile (`family`, `work`, `routine`, `lastSeen`), they add hidden leads and red herrings but **no new answers**. None of them is a proof, and none feeds a deduction.

**Hidden leads (all consistent with the true story):**
- Mrs Pell heard a car stop on Quay Road "some time after ten". This matches the car at 22:21.
- The doctor notes little blood for a scalp wound and blames the rain. But the rain stopped at 21:40, before her own estimated time of death.
- The inventory lists the boots "side by side, toes down", and the watch and earrings untouched. That is odd for a robbery.
- Iris avoided the Saltmarket Stairs and took a tram or taxi after dark (victim `routine`). Her stairs are nowhere near her way home (briefing). Hanna asked twice whether Iris had "really been walking".
- Whitlock recruited Iris and is her line manager (victim `work`). This matches Dolores's "He's been good to me".
- Whitlock volunteers "a difficult claimant" on the phone at 07:25. This is his first push towards Petra.

**Red herrings:**
- Two bag snatches on Mercer Street this month. The robber is a cyclist, both victims were unhurt, and the bags were found in the harbour with the cash taken. Nothing like a dragged, neatly placed body with the watch and earrings left.
- A cigarette end on step 31: old and sodden.
- No answer at Saltmarket Lane no. 9 (Petra).

## Why each wrong suspect fails

- **Marcus Bell (ex-partner).** *Why suspect him:* a loud row the night before; "You'll regret this" both shouted and texted; a lie about when he last saw her; no alibi after 23:04; and the camera shows him at the foot of the stairs at 23:16. *Why he fails:* his lie was embarrassment, and he had apologised by text at 22:19. Iris had promised him his half of the boat. His clock card and initialled grill tickets cover 19:30–21:30 on a short-staffed line with no breaks. At 23:16 she had already lain on the dark landing for 45 minutes. The restaurant is west of the stairs (map 120,480), so his walk home to Eastgate passes their foot.
- **Petra Lund (claimant).** *Why suspect her:* €340,000 at stake, a public shouting match on Tuesday, a lie ("home all night"), and the camera at 23:05. *Why she fails:* the lie was panic (she lives on Saltmarket Lane). Thirty-one signatures and the minutes put her in the Co-op Hall 19:30–21:50, speaking at 20:35. Her claim was not the fraud; Northline cheated her too.
- **Nico Varga (colleague).** *Why suspect him:* he lied about leaving at six, was the last badge-holder out before Iris came back, and admits Iris humiliated him over a file last month. *Why he fails:* he badged out at 19:52, before Iris returned at 19:58. The lie hid a job interview, and his corrected account is specific and checkable.
- **Red herrings:** the missing handbag (staged robbery); the dummy camera at the Anchor & Lamp; the Harbour Club alibi (true, but it starts after the death); Whitlock's helpful pointing at Petra and Marcus.
