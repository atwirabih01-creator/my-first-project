## History training - TEAM DECISION RULE (after rounds 1-3, 27 Oct 2026)

## PRE-DECLARED TEAM RULE for the confirmation rounds 6-11 (fixed 27 Oct 2026, before those rounds are made)
Owner's rule: the team trades only when all three agree.
- **Hawk says TAKE** (chart).
- **Atlas does NOT say "warns"** (context: supports or neutral).
- **Sage's hard limits pass:** at most one coin per BTC moment, at most 3 open at once, loss if wrong ≤ 3× target.

## CONFIRMATION RESULT, rounds 6–11 (scored 27 Oct 2026; rule fixed in advance)
- **Result:** 360 new blind cases. The pre-declared team rule (Hawk TAKE + Atlas not "warns") gave 40 trades,
  80% wins and $1.03 per $1 lost (90% range $0.57–2.38): about break-even. All cases: 77% wins, $0.98.
- **The win rate target (70%) was met. The profit target ($1.20) was NOT.**
- **Atlas's labels flipped:** "warns" made money here ($1.52) and "supports" lost ($0.86). His context read
  from the blind charts is noise, not skill.
- **Hawk's TAKEs:** 80% wins, $1.03. A small edge at best, not proven.
- **Why:** with a 2× ATR stop and a 0.75× ATR target, a loss is about 2.7× a win, so ~73% wins only breaks
  even. The charts alone don't tell the winners from the big losers well enough.
- **Lesson:** rounds 1–5 looked great partly because the rules were tuned on cases already seen. Only fixed
  rules on new cases count.
- **Next:** add new information the charts lack (futures open interest and the long/short ratios, free from
  Binance), then confirm the same way.

## 2026-10-27 · Call "ETH down 24h" from 2,316 · WRONG (ETH 2,362, +2.0%)
- **Expected:** a fall, driven by ETF outflows, lower highs and risk-off before the Fed.
- **Happened:** a broad risk-on bounce. Oil eased, earnings were strong and chip stocks rallied. BTC got back
  above $80k and ETH led the rebound (Moneycontrol, BitcoinInsider, CoinDesk, 27–28 Oct).
- **Verdict:** bad decision. We bet against a rising BTC while ETH was already oversold, which breaks our own
  24 Oct lesson ("sellers exhausted → skip the short").
- **Lessons:**
  - ETF flows lag price. Don't use them alone as a 24h signal.
  - Don't call a coin down when it is oversold and BTC is turning up.
