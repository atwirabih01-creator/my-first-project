## Refined rules
1. **Quiet base, then one loud bar.** The best breakdown shorts come out of a tight, low-volume base
   (volume under 1x for several bars) and break in ONE bar on 4x+ volume, with the target within about
   0.4% of that bar's low (04, 06, 22). Skip when the move is already the second big bar or the bar alone
   is more than ~5% (02, 18): the bounce can reach the stop before the target.
2. **Dip buys need a rejection, not just a low.** No dip buy when the signal bar closes in its bottom 40%
   (03, 16, 21, 26, 27, 29 all did). Wait for a close at 45%+ at a named prior base.
