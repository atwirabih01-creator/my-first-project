## Chart rules I am using
1. Second-bar-of-a-flush rule: after a move already larger than ~5% in 1-2 bars on 5x+ volume, skip. The target then
   needs a NEW extreme, while the stop sits inside the normal half-retracement bounce (08, 09, 11, 14, 17, 18, 19).
2. Stop must sit beyond the level that proves the idea wrong. If a plain retest of the just-broken level would hit
   the stop, skip (11, 17). If the stop is beyond a real ceiling/floor that held before, that is a plus (03, 06, 20).
3. Fade only breaks that show exhaustion: weak or falling volume, an upper/lower wick, shrinking bar ranges, or a
   known level overhead. Skip breaks that close at the extreme with rising volume or come cleanly out of a tight
   base (01, 13, 16, 21) - those are the ones that squeeze.
4. Target placement: best when it lies inside the signal bar's own range or the prior bar (no level in the way).
   Lower grade when it sits in a rejection wick zone (07) or exactly on a nearby shelf (15).
5. Fee check: our rule says target at least 3 x 0.30% = 0.90%. Most targets here are 0.42-0.83%; if that rule
   applies to these practice trades, only 01, 07, 08, 09, 11, 12, 14, 16, 17, 19, 21, 23 pass it. Sage should
   decide whether the replay already counts fees; 24 (0.42%) is the clearest problem.
