## General observations
1. Three families of setup: (a) fading a rally in a long-term downtrend (02,03,04,05,10,12,13,15,21,22,24),
   (b) selling into a breakdown (06,07,08,09,11,14,17,18), (c) buying a dip in an uptrend (01,16,19,20,23).
   Context matters differently for each, so read BTC against the family, not in general.
2. Fades look worst when BTC itself is jumping in the last 1-4 bars (02, 03): the coin's rally is the market,
   not the coin. Fades look best when the coin pumped alone and BTC is flat (21).
3. Breakdown shorts and dip buys share one danger: entering after the move is spent - RSI under ~22, 48-bar move
   beyond ~-10%, or signal volume above ~8x (11, 14, 18, 19). Per lessons.md (24 Oct), when sellers look
   exhausted, either allow a long hold or skip.
4. Clustering: 08/09/18 are the same BTC moment, and 11/17 are another. Losses would come together; taking all
   of a cluster multiplies one bad read. Suggest at most one trade per BTC moment.
5. Thin evidence: BTC closes only (no BTC volume/high-low), 24 bars, and no news or funding/positioning data.
   Context alone is a weak filter here - I would use "warns" to require Hawk/Sage agreement, not as an automatic skip.
