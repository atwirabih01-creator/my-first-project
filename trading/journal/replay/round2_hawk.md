## Chart rules refined this round
1. **The target must sit close to the signal bar's own low (or high).** The best breakdown shorts need price to go only
   about 0.3% or less past the signal bar's extreme (19, 20, 22, 29). If the target sits inside a long wick that buyers
   already pushed back (01, 17, 28, 30), skip: that wick shows buyers stepping in, not clear space.
2. **The stop must sit beyond the broken level, not inside the old range.** Good: above the last bar before the flush,
   or above broken support (18, 20, 22). Skip when the stop sits inside earlier chop (01, 24, 30), because ordinary
   noise can hit it before the idea is proven wrong.
3. **Dip buys and fades need a named level plus a rejection.** Buy a dip only at a prior base, with the low rejected
   (close at or above 45% of the bar, like 23). Never fade a steady staircase that closes in its top 10% (27),
   even when BTC is flat. A close mid-range after a flush (40% or more) means the sellers are spent, so skip it.
