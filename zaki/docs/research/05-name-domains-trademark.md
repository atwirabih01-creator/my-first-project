# 05: Name availability, domains, app-store names and trademarks for "Zaki" (ذكي)

**Date checked:** 2026-10-15. **Scope:** Qatar originally, widened by the founder to the Middle East and GCC, with the **first pilot in Lebanon** and a native app on the Apple App Store and Google Play.
**Method:** Live lookups from the shell: domain registries (RDAP, the modern version of "whois"), Google public DNS, Apple's public App Store search, and Google Play pages. Also web searches and official or registrar web pages. Nothing was registered, bought, reserved or contacted.
**Not legal advice.** A trademark lawyer must run a proper clearance search before filing.

Status labels: **Confirmed** (checked directly at the source), **Partly confirmed** (strong sign but not a direct registry answer, or a second-hand source), **Not found** (searched, nothing came up), **Could not check**.

---

## Plain-language summary

- **"Zaki" by itself is too crowded to own.** There are at least 15 apps called Zaki or Zaky in the Lebanon, Qatar, Saudi, UAE, Egypt and Jordan app stores. One of them is a **children's learning app called "Zaki" (ages 2–7)** on Google Play, launched in 2026. It sits in the same space as our app.
- **In Qatar, Qatar Islamic Bank already has an AI assistant called "Zaki".** It speaks Arabic and English and is still running in 2026. Parents in Doha may already know that name as "the bank's chatbot".
- **In Lebanon, where the pilot runs, a payments app called "zaky" exists.** It is made by areeba, a well-known Lebanese payments company, and was updated as recently as October 2026.
- **The exact App Store name "Zaki" is taken.** Apple does not allow two apps with the same name. So we need a longer store name such as **"Zaki: Homework Helper"** or **"Hey Zaki – Homework Tutor"**. Every combination we tested returned no matching apps.
- **The Arabic word ذكي just means "smart".** Dozens of apps use it ("the smart teacher", "smart preparation"). Trademark offices usually refuse to give one company ownership of an everyday word like "smart" for a smart-learning product. On its own, it would be weak protection.
- **Good domains are still free:** heyzaki.app, zakitutor.com, zakikids.com, zakitutor.app, askzaki.app, zakihomework.com, zakitutor.ai, and the Qatar, Saudi and Lebanon country versions (zaki.qa, zaki.sa, zaki.lb). **Taken:** zaki.com, zaki.ai, zaki.app, zaki.ae, askzaki.com, heyzaki.com, zakiapp.com, meetzaki.com, zakilearn.com, zaki.academy (taken by the kids app).
- **Verdict: AMBER.** We can keep "Zaki" as the friendly character's name. The **brand we register and publish should be a two-part name plus a mascot logo**, for example "Hey Zaki" or "Zaki Tutor". A lawyer should do a proper search in Lebanon, Qatar, Saudi Arabia and the UAE before we spend money on branding.
- **If the founder wants a name the company can fully own across the region, now is the cheapest time to rename.** It costs nothing before launch. Two-part "Zaki" names are workable but will always share the space with other "Zaki"s.

## What this changes in the plan

1. **Section 2, decision #1 ("no education-app conflicts found") is out of date.** There is now a kids' learning app called "Zaki" (Google Play, ages 2–7, site zaki.academy). There is also a long-running children's Islamic learning series, "Zaky & Friends" (One4Kids). Suggested new wording: *"Zaki is the character name. The registered brand is a two-part name (working choice: 'Hey Zaki'), pending lawyer clearance."*
2. **Section 9, item 4 (trademark):** widen it from Qatar to **Lebanon (pilot), Qatar, Saudi Arabia and the UAE**. File for the **two-part name plus logo**, not the single word "Zaki" or "ذكي". Classes to cover: **9** (the downloadable app), **41** (education and tutoring), and **42** (online software or AI service). Use the Madrid system (one international application covering several countries) only after a lawyer confirms which countries it reaches. Lebanon is **not** a Madrid member (see 3.4), so it needs its own national filing.
3. **Section 9, item 5 (domains and handles):** replace the list with the "Recommended set" in section 1.4 below. **zaki.ai is confirmed taken.** Main regional website: a **.com or .app** name, because country domains need a local company.
4. **Phase 4 (launch prep), new item:** choose the store name, such as "Hey Zaki: Homework Tutor". Reserve it in App Store Connect and Google Play Console once the developer account exists. That step is free but needs the owner's approval (CLAUDE.md "ask first").
5. **Section 11 (market):** add QIB "Zaki" (Qatar), areeba "zaky" (Lebanon), "Zaki – AI Agent & Assistant" (Saudi developer) and "Zaki kids" (Google Play) as **name neighbours**. None of them is a direct competitor, but all can cause confusion.
6. **Pronunciation note for the brand team:** in the Gulf, ذكي sounds like "dhaki" or "thaki". In Lebanon, Egypt and Jordan most people say "zaki". Written in Latin letters, "Zaki" is also a common man's name (زكي, meaning "pure"). The Arabic logo should therefore always show **ذكي**, so the meaning "smart" is clear.

**Who can register country domains (based mostly on registrar pages, so a registrar should confirm before buying):**

- **Qatar (.qa, .com.qa).** Run by the Communications Regulatory Authority (CRA). domains.qa now redirects to https://www.cra.gov.qa/en/Services/Domains, which has a "Check Domain Availability" page. Registration goes through **CRA-accredited registrars**, which since late 2024 must be companies registered in Qatar. Plain **.qa** is described as open, though one registrar says you need a Qatar ID or residence permit (*Partly confirmed*). **.com.qa needs a Qatari Commercial Registration (CR)**. Sources: https://www.aeserver.com/support/qa-domain-name-policy-explained, https://qhost.qa/how-to-register-a-qa-domain (checked 2026-10-15). Note: the plan's e-commerce licence item (Section 9.1) needs a .qa domain, so the founder will want **zaki.qa or heyzaki.qa** once the company exists.
- **Lebanon (.lb, .com.lb).** Run by LBDR, through accredited registrars, with a Lebanese postal address and **a registered Lebanese company or organisation** (a paid "local presence" service is possible). Since Feb 2021 a Lebanese trademark is reportedly **no longer** required for .com.lb; a trademark from any country can show your right to the name (*Partly confirmed*: sources disagree). Sources: https://lbdr.org.lb/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/LBDR_Domain_Name_Policy_Main_A_20210209.pdf, https://domgate.com/whois-and-domain-registration/.com.lb, https://news.gandi.net/?p=80682.
- **Saudi Arabia (.sa, .com.sa).** Run by SaudiNIC. You need a **Saudi company registration matching the name, or a Saudi trademark** matching the name, plus a local contact. One registrar says new .sa registrations are paused (*Partly confirmed*). Sources: https://support.nominus.com/hc/en-us/articles/360022776134, https://www.agip.com/Agip-Country-Service/en/120/DN/2.
- **UAE (.ae).** Run by aeDA/TDRA. Plain .ae is reportedly open to individuals. **.co.ae needs a UAE trade licence or UAE trademark**. zaki.ae is already taken anyway. Source: https://www.aeserver.com/ae-domain-name-policy-explained/.

## 3. Trademarks

### 3.1 What could be checked automatically

- **WIPO Global Brand Database** (https://branddb.wipo.int): **Could not check.** It shows a "prove you're human" puzzle (Altcha) before any results, so automated searching is blocked. Checked 2026-10-15.
- **Qatar, Saudi, UAE and Lebanon national registers:** **Could not check.** All need a login or a paid inquiry.
- **Indirect evidence found:** the "Zaki" app by **Zaki Universal Inc.** says in its App Store description: *"ZAKI®, a registered trademark of Zaki Universal Inc."* (charitable giving and payments; country of registration not stated). https://apps.apple.com/sa/app/zaki/id6749177228 (*Partly confirmed*: the company's own claim).

### 3.2 Step-by-step: WIPO Global Brand Database (free, covers many countries)

1. Go to https://branddb.wipo.int and switch the language to English or Arabic.
2. Complete the "I'm not a robot" check.
3. In the search box choose **Brand name**. Type `zaki` and search. Then repeat with `zaky`, `zakee`, `ذكي` and `زكي` (the Arabic spellings).
4. On the left, filter **Designation / Office** to: **Qatar (QA), Saudi Arabia (SA), United Arab Emirates (AE), Egypt (EG), Jordan (JO), Lebanon (LB), Kuwait, Oman, Bahrain**, plus **WIPO (Madrid)** and **EUIPO**.
5. Filter **Nice class** to **9, 41, 42** (also look at 16 and 28 for books and toys).
6. Change the search mode to **"Fuzzy" or "Phonetic"** to catch look-alikes such as "Zakki" or "Zakey".
7. For each result, note the **brand, owner, country, classes, and status** (Active/Pending/Expired). Take a screenshot.
8. Repeat with your full brand candidates: `hey zaki`, `zaki tutor`.
   Not every Gulf office sends full data to WIPO, so also do the national searches below.

### 3.3 Qatar (Ministry of Commerce and Industry)

1. Do the free WIPO search (3.2) with **Qatar** selected as the office.
2. For an official answer, use MoCI's **trademark inquiry/search service** (about **QAR 1,000** per a 2026 guide). Log in at https://www.moci.gov.qa with **Tawtheeq/NAS (national login)**, go to **E-services → Trademarks**, and choose the search/inquiry service.
3. Filing (later, through a lawyer): about **QAR 4,500 per class** in total (filing 1,000 + publication 500 + registration 3,000), *Partly confirmed*, sources disagree. https://www.qatarliving.com/en/article/trademarks-patents-qatar-business-ip; MoCI online-registration guidance: https://thepeninsulaqatar.com/article/24/08/2025/moci-outlines-guidelines-for-registering-trademarks-online (checked 2026-10-15).

### 3.4 Lebanon (Ministry of Economy and Trade, Intellectual Property Protection Office): pilot country

1. The Office is at the Ministry of Economy and Trade (https://www.economy.gov.lb). Lebanon **is not part of the Madrid system**, so international filings don't reach it; you need a **national filing** (*Partly confirmed* from IP-firm guides).
2. A public online database of Lebanese marks reportedly exists, but we could not find a working link. In practice, **searches are done by a local IP lawyer or agent**, about **USD 110 for the first class + 100 for each extra class**, and searches only cover the words (not logos). Arabic-script searches may cost extra. https://www.ip-coster.com/IPGuides/trademark-lebanon (checked 2026-10-15).
3. Ask the lawyer to search: `zaki`, `zaky`, `ذكي`, `زكي`, `hey zaki` in classes **9, 41, 42**. Also ask specifically about **areeba SAL's "zaky"** mark and which classes it covers.
4. New filings are published in the Official Gazette with a 30-day objection period.

### 3.5 Saudi Arabia (SAIP)

1. Go to https://www.saip.gov.sa. SAIP launched a **free public search tool** for trademarks in March 2024 (https://www.worldtrademarkreview.com/article/ip-rights-new-search-tool-launched-saudi-authority-intellectual-property).
2. Open **E-services → IP Search** (often at ipsearch.saip.gov.sa; *Partly confirmed*), choose **Trademarks**, and search the word in Arabic and English. Then filter classes 9, 41 and 42.
3. Saudi law firms advise adding a **professional search**, because the free tool can miss sound-alike marks (https://atblegal.com/blog/intellectual-property-laww/trademark-registration-ksa/).

### 3.6 UAE (Ministry of Economy)

1. Go to https://www.moec.gov.ae → **E-services → Trademarks → "Trademark search/inquiry"**. Log in with **UAE Pass**.
2. Enter the word and the class (9, 41, 42). The fee is either free or about **AED 350**; sources disagree. https://thaonco.com/translation-times/legal/uae-trademark-search/, https://virtuzone.com/blog/trademark-search-uae/ (checked 2026-10-15).

### 3.7 The "ذكي = smart" problem (important)

ذكي is an everyday Arabic word meaning "smart". It already appears in dozens of app names: "المعلم الذكي" (The Smart Teacher, Education, 1,573 ratings), "التحضير الذكي" (Smart Preparation, Education), "قارئ الأطفال الذكي" (Smart Kids Reader), "منصة ذكي" and others (Apple App Store search, Qatar/Saudi/UAE/Egypt, 2026-10-15). Trademark offices usually **refuse or limit** marks that just describe the product. A "smart" app for "smart learning" is a typical example. **Expect "ذكي" alone to be hard to register and almost impossible to enforce against others in classes 9 and 41.** A distinctive two-part name, plus the mascot drawn as a logo, is much stronger.

## 5. App Store and Google Play names

- **Apple App Store:** app names must be unique. An app named exactly **"Zaki" already exists** (Zaki Universal Inc., id6749177228), so **"Zaki" alone is not available** (*Confirmed* by store search). Searches for "zaki tutor" and "zaki homework" in the Lebanon, US and UK stores returned **no matching apps**.
- **Google Play:** names don't have to be unique, but "Zaki kids" already uses Zaki in Education, so a plain "Zaki" listing would be confused with it.
- **Suggested store names** (up to 30 characters on Apple; final availability is only certain when you type it into App Store Connect):
  1. **Hey Zaki: Homework Tutor** (24 characters): recommended; matches heyzaki.app
  2. **Zaki Tutor: Homework Helper** (27)
  3. **Zaki: Homework Teacher** (22)
  4. Arabic store name (Apple lets you set one per language): **ذكي – مساعد الواجبات** ("Zaki – homework helper")
- In the store's subtitle and keywords, include "ذكي" and "Zaki" so people searching either word find us.

## 6. Verdict

| Use | Rating | Why |
|---|---|---|
| "Zaki" as the **mascot or character name** inside the app | **Green** | A character name is fine. It is the plain meaning of the word and is not used as the trademark. |
| **"Zaki" alone** as the registered brand, App Store name, or main domain, anywhere in the region | **Red** | The App Store name is taken. zaki.com, zaki.ai, zaki.app and zaki.ae are taken. It is likely registrable as a combined word-and-logo mark, but it will share the space with other Zakis. **A lawyer's clearance search in LB, QA, SA and AE is needed before spending on branding.** |
| Overall regional verdict for "Zaki" | **AMBER** | Workable with a two-part brand. If the founder wants a name the company can fully own across the GCC, renaming now costs nothing. |

**Fallback naming pattern (in order):** `Hey Zaki` → `Zaki Tutor` → `Ask Zaki`, always with the mascot logo and ذكي in Arabic. If the lawyer finds a blocking mark (most likely from "Zaki kids" in class 41/9 or a Gulf filing), keep the mascot named Zaki and pick a new, invented company or app name. That kind of name is easier to own.
